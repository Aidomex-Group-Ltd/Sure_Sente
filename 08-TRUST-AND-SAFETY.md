# Trust, moderation and disputes

## Verification levels
Phone possession; identity check; business check; listing authority check; category credential check. Each has independent status, reviewer, date, expiry and scope. Paying a fee never completes a check. Public badges explain what was checked and its limits.

## Listing review
Ordinary listings may use rule-based screening plus report review. High-value or restricted categories require pre-publication review. Reject misleading earnings, illegal goods, recruitment-fee scams, deceptive commissions, harassment, exploitation and fabricated identity evidence.

## Worker protections
Visible pay and scope before acceptance; no pay-to-apply; no discriminatory targeting; precise locations private until needed; block/report from listing and chat; allow leaving unsafe work and opening a dispute. Adults-only pilot. Categories with hazardous work require additional screening before activation.

## Dispute workflow
Open case → acknowledge → collect both accounts/evidence → temporary action if needed → reasoned platform decision → notify → appeal → close. Proposed pilot SLA: acknowledgement within one business day, triage urgent reports promptly during stated support hours. Never promise 24/7 support without staffing.

Case types: nonpayment, duplicate referral, changed terms, failed service, misleading listing, harassment, impersonation, refund. Reporter sees progress; other users see only necessary information. Keep accused user's response and avoid automatic punishment from a single unverified report.

## Moderation actions
Request changes; pause listing; restrict new actions; suspend account; restore; close case. Require staff reason, actor, timestamp, duration and appeal info. Separate fraud review from support; finance-related changes require privileged roles.

## Privacy and security
Minimise identity and buyer data; private upload storage; malware/file checks; short-lived download URLs; access logs; retention schedule by data purpose. Never store full identity documents in public media. Do not train models on private chats/documents without a separately approved basis.

## Reviews
Only confirmed transaction participants can review. One review per participant per transaction, with report/appeal support. No purchased positive reviews; no incentives conditioned on positive ratings.
