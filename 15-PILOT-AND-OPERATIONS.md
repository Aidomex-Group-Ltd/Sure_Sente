# Pilot and operating model

## Proposed pilot
One locality chosen by founder access; recruit paying demand first. Illustrative target: 10 businesses/households, 20 workers/providers, 5 sellers and 5 authorised brokers. These are planning targets, not traction claims.

## Discovery questions
What did you last pay someone to do? How did you find them? Where do agreements fail? Do you pay introducers? What establishes entitlement? Would you run one real paid transaction through this workflow? Who handles support and disputes?

## Onboarding
Founder helps create first listings and teaches confirmation/payment states. Do not import contact lists without consent. Verify published opportunities with posters. Allow manual matching during pilot and label staff assistance honestly.

## Weekly dashboard
Completed paid tasks; amounts acknowledged received; confirmed commission receipts; repeat customers; employer response time; unresolved disputes; form abandonment; provider/SMS costs. Separate estimates and reported values from confirmed amounts. Avoid claiming losses prevented or income generated from views/sign-ups.

## Commercial test
Worker applications free. Quote business subscription and setup/training fee after observing workflow. Test one pricing model at a time. Broker commission paid by seller under separate terms. Sponsored listings clearly labelled. No automatic paid renewal without clear acceptance.

## Support
Published operating hours and response targets; channels; incident owner; triage; evidence privacy; appeal path. Emergency page directs users to appropriate local services, not unsupported app emergency promises.

## Expansion criteria
Reliable demand, repeat usage, low unresolved dispute rate, sustainable support cost and evidence that users get paid. Expand area/category separately so failure sources remain observable.
