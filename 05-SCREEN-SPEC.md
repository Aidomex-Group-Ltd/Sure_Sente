Dining set • UGX 800,000 asking price • Commission UGX 40,000 per completed sale • Seller-authorised brokers only. Button: View terms.

## Trust labels
Phone confirmed means phone possession only. Identity checked requires completed check. Seller authority checked describes evidence reviewed, not ownership/title guaranteed. Sponsored describes paid placement, not quality.

## Confirmation copy
You are accepting [scope] for [amount and currency], payable [timing], under offer version [version]. Review before accepting.
Broker: Your commission is [amount/rule]. It becomes earned when [trigger]. Payment is due [deadline]. Existing customers and duplicate introductions follow [rule].

## Avoid
Guaranteed income, instant verified after payment, wallet balance for unreceived money, and permanent urgency timers. Never silently send messages or promote listings on a user's behalf.
