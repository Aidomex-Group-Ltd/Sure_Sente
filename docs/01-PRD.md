# Product requirements

## Problem and purpose
Informal workers need visible paid opportunities without a formal CV. Customers need clear prices, reliable contact, and evidence of completion. Introducers need an agreed record of referrals and commissions. These are hypotheses to validate in a pilot, not established market-size claims.

## Core outcomes
- Worker can discover a nearby opportunity, understand pay, and apply in a few steps.
- Customer can post a need, compare providers, and confirm a booking.
- Seller can authorise brokers and publish commission terms.
- Broker can register a consented introduction and track its outcome.
- Participants can report problems and retrieve agreed terms and receipts.

## First-release exclusions
No job guarantees, automated lending, investment returns, unrestricted recruitment abroad, custody of client funds, algorithmic worker rejection, or required payment to apply. No paid verification badge that implies an identity check occurred.

## Scope of brokerage
Commission arises from an actual qualifying sale, rental or service booking under accepted terms. No recruitment/downline rewards. Membership invitations alone do not earn sales commission. Brokering a work vacancy does not create a candidate recruitment fee.

## Proposed monetisation
Worker browsing and applications remain free. Test business subscriptions, promoted listings marked Sponsored, and explicitly disclosed completed-booking fees. Direct seller-to-broker commission is separate from platform revenue. Fees must be agreed before a transaction.

## Success measures
Paid completions, confirmed commission payments, repeat paying customers, time to first employer response, unresolved disputes, notification delivery, and mobile form completion. Sign-ups alone are not evidence of income creation.

## Guest-first update (12 Oct 2026)
Rule: "Browse all public content freely; authenticate when taking a protected action." Guests browse all published listings, public profiles, public reviews, commission terms, search/filters, help and policy pages with no account. Account required only at the action boundary (apply, message, quote, book, publish, broker request/agreement, referral, personal records). Gated categories stay disabled for guests and users alike.
