# Country and category launch gates

This document is an implementation checklist, not a statement of legal clearance. Current requirements must be verified with appropriate local authorities, licensed providers and qualified advisers before activation. This scaffold does not assert licences, registration or legal compliance.

## Uganda base gate
Confirm working-brand availability; company operating identity; privacy notices and applicable data obligations; participant terms; commission agreement; complaints contacts; tax/receipt responsibilities; supported payment-provider terms. Record reviewer, source, date, decision and next review.

## Category gates
| Category | Evidence to resolve before enabling |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary work/services | Adult eligibility, fair listing rules, employment-versus-contractor terms, safety and dispute handling |
| Goods | Seller authority, prohibited goods, condition/delivery/refund disclosures |
| Vehicles | Ownership/agency evidence, inspection disclosures, transfer process and intermediary requirements |
| Property/land | Authority to market, relevant intermediary rules, title-check responsibilities, fees and contract process |
| Hire purchase/rent-to-own | Financing structure, licensed partner if required, total costs, default/ownership terms and consumer protections |
| Finance/insurance | Professional permissions, promotion/referral rules, fee structure and data handling |
| Health | Provider credentials, restricted products, privacy and clinical boundaries |
| Overseas recruitment | Agency approvals, employer/job-order checks, destination requirements and worker-fee protections |
| Payments/escrow | Provider authorisation, custody structure, settlement, refunds, disputes and reconciliation |

## International expansion
Country-specific currency, language, locations, contracts, work rules, tax documents, privacy, payment rails and category configuration. Uganda approval is not approval elsewhere. Keep disabled by default until country gate is recorded.

## Launch rule
No placeholder acceptance such as compliant=true. Release checklist requires actual evidence and human approval. This prevents unsupported claims while allowing ordinary pilot scope to move forward.
